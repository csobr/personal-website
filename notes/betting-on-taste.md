Taste is judgment. Judgment needs training data. If you're online too much, your training data is the same as everyone "like you." Same data, same judgment, same output.

Spend enough time on any platform and your opinions become downstream of it. Twitter-brain, Pinterest-brain. Influenced and predictable from the feed. If it's predictable, it's tradable. Someone is betting on what you'll like next. They're probably right.

Cory Doctorow described the modern internet as "five giant websites filled with screenshots of the other four." This but for Pinterest. Open Instagram, TikTok, Substack. The pins you saw yesterday are now on the four other apps. The same cute bag charms. Photos from influencer dinners. Close up shots of candles with bows on them.

Cereal-box curation, as I like to call it. Everyone's a curator of the 4U page. There's a 4U page for every micro-aesthetic.

![A mock prediction market titled "Will Poetcore be in your saved pins by March?" — Yes and No contracts priced at $213 and $87, a 47% chance line charted from October to January, and 2.3M saves this month in trend data.](/images/notes/harmshi.webp)

By the time it reaches you, it's echo. You register it. You apply it. Now it's noise. You alongside ten thousand others. Another data point in the trend report.

If your taste aligns with something like Pinterest Predictions you're not cultivating it. You're being cultivated. Your taste follows a 4U page. You're not upstream of culture but downstream of it.

Taste can be consensus. But if you formed it after the consensus, you're not tastemaking.

Get less 4U brained for your own benefit.

— Siham
