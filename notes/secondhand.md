Shopping more secondhand has affected me in ways I did not expect. I'm reflecting on the stories behind the items which often lead to reminders about loss and gain.

When shopping fast fashion, a shirt made in Bangladesh is just not potent enough to trigger these reflections. That long, ugly journey is far-fetched, in every sense, compared to the short journey of the piece of cream Dala-floda embroidered cloth in my entryway that I just picked up at an estate sale, belonging to a woman who took her last breath on this Dunya, nine days ago.

On actively building the best internet secondhand sourcing scout, and it leading to increased bidding at auctions. There are more losses than gains. To the tune of Be Mine by Robyn. Seeing the promise of something being yours. Sourcing a rare lot and losing the bid, or choosing to walk away, knowing that this is not the time for me to own this. That one item, with a special provenance that would have become part of mine. It never was, and probably never will be, mine.

— Siham
